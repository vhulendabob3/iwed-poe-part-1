our story

Hetero Ghetto started in 2021 with a goal to bring kasi style back to the hood, with quality fabrics

The dusty streets of Soweto were quiet, but inside Owen’s small garage , a vibrant symphony of printing of machines was just beginning to hum. In his hand was a pair of vintage hoodie jeans, transformed with patches of bright Shweshwe print, ready to be paired with a crisp white crewneck bearing the logo "HETERO GHETTO".
